Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Who logs in with Google

Only the app's owner logs in with Google, once, with the YouTube account that owns the playlists. Nobody else is asked to log in with Google, and the app reads no data from anyone else's Google account.

What the app does with YouTube access

The app creates playlists on the owner's YouTube account and adds or removes videos in them. It also searches YouTube for videos that match songs. It does nothing else with the account.

What is stored

The app keeps the login token for the owner's account on its own server, so it can update the playlists. It also keeps the IDs of matched videos and of the playlists it made. This data is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Google API Services

Music Shelf's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then stops updating the playlists.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/teriba/music-shelf/issues.
